Interactive mapping of the OJT Portal network. Drag nodes to explore relationships.

Portal Relationships

adminsystem.html (Admin Portal)

The central hub of the system. It governs global settings, manages user accounts across all other portals, and acts as the highest level of authorization. Connects directly to TSD, IC, and Schooling portals to configure their operational parameters.

tsdportal.html (Training Systems Dept)

Manages the overall training lifecycle. Connects to the IC portal for company deployments, the Schooling portal for academic progress, and directly monitors Trainee milestones. It is the bridge between the academic and on-the-job training phases.

ic-portal.html (Industry Coordinator)

Facilitates the partnership between Dualtech and partner companies. ICs use this to monitor trainees deployed in the field. It links heavily to the Mentoring portal to collaborate with company supervisors and the Trainee portal for attendance and performance tracking.

mentoring.html (Mentor Portal)

Used by supervisors inside partner companies. Connects directly to the Trainee portal to evaluate their performance, input grades, and track tasks. Coordinates with the IC portal to report on trainee status.

schooling.html (Schooling Portal)

Handles the in-school phase of the dual training system. Links to the Trainee portal to manage their academic grades, school attendance, and requirements before or during their deployment phase.

trainee.html (Trainee Portal)

The endpoint for the student. It receives data from Mentoring (OJT grades), Schooling (school grades), and is monitored by both ICs and TSD. The trainee uses this to view schedules, upload requirements, and track their dual training journey.

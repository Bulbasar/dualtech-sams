Republic Act No. 10173 — Data Privacy Act of 2012

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) of the Philippines, we are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

What we collect:
We collect your Student Number, full name, section, adviser, proctor, training status, contact details (Messenger link, email address, phone number), and account credentials.

Purpose of collection:
Your data is collected solely for the purpose of managing your attendance records and facilitating communication between trainees and program coordinators.
